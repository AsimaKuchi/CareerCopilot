# JobMatch AI Browser Extension

A Chrome extension that automatically fills job application forms with your tailored resume and cover letter from JobMatch AI.

## Features

- **One-Click Auto-Fill**: Fill job application forms instantly
- **Smart Field Detection**: Recognizes common form fields (name, email, resume, cover letter)
- **Greenhouse Support**: Optimized for Greenhouse ATS job boards
- **Floating Button**: Convenient auto-fill button on job application pages
- **Secure**: Uses your existing JobMatch AI session

## Installation

### For Development/Testing:

1. Open Chrome and go to `chrome://extensions/`
2. Enable "Developer mode" (toggle in top right)
3. Click "Load unpacked"
4. Select the `browser-extension` folder
5. The extension icon will appear in your toolbar

### For Users (when published):

1. Visit the Chrome Web Store (coming soon)
2. Click "Add to Chrome"
3. Log in to JobMatch AI

## How to Use

1. **Log in to JobMatch AI** at https://jobfinder-74.preview.emergentagent.com
2. **Find a job** and save it to your applications
3. **Generate** your tailored resume and cover letter
4. **Approve** the application
5. **Navigate** to the job's application page (Greenhouse, Lever, etc.)
6. **Click** the floating "Auto-Fill" button OR the extension icon
7. **Review** the filled fields
8. **Upload** your resume file if required
9. **Submit** the application

## Troubleshooting

**"Please log in to JobMatch AI first"**
- Make sure you're logged in at https://jobfinder-74.preview.emergentagent.com

**Form fields not filling**
- Some custom forms may have non-standard field names
- Use the manual copy buttons in JobMatch AI as a fallback

**File upload required**
- The extension cannot upload files automatically
- Download your .docx resume from JobMatch AI and upload manually

## Privacy

- The extension only activates on supported job board websites
- Your data is fetched securely from your JobMatch AI account
- No data is stored locally or sent to third parties
